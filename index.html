Privacy Policy

Patriot Daily Quotes  ·  Last updated August 12, 2026

Patriot Daily Quotes is designed to work without collecting personal information about you. This policy explains what that means in practice, and describes the one exception: subscription purchases.

Information the app does not collect

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. You are never asked for your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying information.

The app contains no analytics or tracking software. It does not record which quotes you read, how often you open the app, or how you use it. It does not use an advertising identifier and contains no advertising.

Information stored on your device

Some information is saved on your phone so the app can work as expected:

  • Your reading streak
  • Which quotes you have already seen, so the back catalog stays accurate
  • Your notification preferences

This information stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to access it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Notifications

Daily quote notifications are scheduled by the app on your device. No information is sent to a server to deliver them, and nothing about your notification activity is reported back to us. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

Subscription purchases

If you subscribe to unlock premium content, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We use a service called RevenueCat to verify that a subscription is active and to keep it working across your devices.

For subscribers, RevenueCat receives a purchase identifier from Google Play, along with basic technical information such as your device type, operating system version, app version, and IP address. This is used only to confirm and manage the subscription.

We never receive your payment card details. Those are handled entirely by Google Play and are never visible to us.

If you do not subscribe, none of this applies to you.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy. Google Play's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Information sharing

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The only information leaving your device is the subscription data described above, and it goes only to the payment and subscription services required to make your subscription work.

Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no accounts and no personal profiles, there is no stored record of you to delete. Information saved on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

If you subscribe and want subscription records associated with your purchase removed, write to the address below and we will process the request.

Children

Patriot Daily Quotes is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to patriotdailyquotes@gmail.com.
